# Platform rules for every idea

- The creator is a fictional adult (21+) AI character. Every post carries the AI label.
  Nothing may refer to anyone under 21.
- Explicit pictures only on Fanvue. Instagram and TikTok get safe-for-work teasers only:
  no nudity, no see-through or "strategic covering" tricks, no codewords.
- No spam, no fake urgency ("only 2 left", "price goes up tonight"), no buying followers,
  no fake engagement.
- Payments and contact stay on Fanvue.
- Every action that posts, messages or spends is for the owner to approve: say so next to it.
