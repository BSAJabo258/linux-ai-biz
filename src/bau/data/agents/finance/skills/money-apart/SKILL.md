# Money kept apart

- **Received** is only what `data.money` says was received (after fees, refunds counted).
- **Prices** (a post's unlock price, a subscription) are not money received.
- **Offers sent and posts queued** may bring money later. They are not received either.
- Never add prices or offers into a total. Never write "expected", "projected" or
  "potential" earnings as a number.
- Write each amount exactly as the tool gave it, with its dollar sign.
- Nothing received is a fact too: say "$0.00 received" plainly.
