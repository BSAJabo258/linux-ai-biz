# Set up the channel (once)

Answer these, then write the answers into `_shared/series-bible.md` in place of every
`{{...}}`. Edit `_shared/voice.md` if you want a different tone.

1. What is the channel called?
2. Which age band: 2-4, 4-6 or 6-8? (Pick one; it changes words, pace and topics.)
3. What should children learn from it? One or two themes.
4. Where do the stories happen?
5. Who are the main characters? For each: name, what they are, how they look (colours,
   size, one feature you'd recognise anywhere), personality, how they talk. They must be
   your own: no characters, names or styles someone else owns.
6. How long is an episode, and what happens in every one (a song, a lesson recap)?

Keep it short: a page is plenty. Every stage reads this file, so a long bible makes
every step slower and less focused.
