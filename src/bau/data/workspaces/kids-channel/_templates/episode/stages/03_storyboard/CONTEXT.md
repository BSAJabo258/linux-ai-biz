# 03_storyboard: scene by scene

One job: turn the script into a numbered list of scenes.

## Inputs
- Working (this run): ../02_script/output/script.md
- Reference (every run): ../../../../_shared/series-bible.md

## Process
1. One numbered scene per `SCENE:` in the script.
2. For each: where it is, who is in it, what happens, the lines spoken, about how many
   seconds it lasts.

## Outputs
- storyboard.md → output/

## Human check
Does every line of the script have a scene? Are the timings close to the episode length?
