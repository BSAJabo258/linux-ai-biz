## Process
1. Write exactly three lines: `Title: ...`, `Description: ...`, `Tags: a, b, c`.
2. Plain title in normal capitals, no "!!!", no emoji strings, not like an earlier title.
3. Description: one or two calm sentences about the story. No links, no calls to action.
