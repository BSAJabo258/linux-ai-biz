# Voice

- Short sentences, simple words a child of the age band knows.
- Warm and calm. Never shouting, never sarcastic.
- The narrator speaks to the child as a friend: "Can you see the red ball?" is fine;
  asking for anything about the child is not.
- Name feelings plainly: happy, sad, worried, proud.
- Repeat the learning goal two or three times, in different words.
- End on something gentle: a song, a hug, a goodnight.
