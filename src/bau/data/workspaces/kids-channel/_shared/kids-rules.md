# Kids rules (every episode)

These are the same rules BAU's publish checks enforce (`kids.py`,
`data/policies/kids_content.yaml`). Following them here saves rework later.

The audience is young children, and the video is marked **made for kids** on YouTube.

Never:
- ask children for anything about themselves: name, age, school, address, photos;
- tell children to comment, like, subscribe, share or turn on notifications;
- include links or send children anywhere off YouTube;
- push buying anything, or tell children to ask a grown-up to buy something;
- include scares, violence, weapons, injections or needles, pranks, kidnapping, or
  anything frightening;
- use characters, names or styles someone else owns (no Peppa, Bluey, Paw Patrol,
  Cocomelon, Disney, Pokemon, Minecraft and so on);
- write a sensational title: no ALL CAPS, no "!!!" or "???", no strings of emoji.

Always:
- one clear, gentle story with one learning goal;
- a story that is different from every earlier episode, not a copy with new names
  (YouTube demonetises mass-produced, repetitive channels);
- calm pacing and kind characters who solve problems together.
