# 01_plan: the week's posts

One job: turn the owner's theme into a plan of posts for the week.

## Inputs

- Reference (every run): ../../../../_shared/persona.md
- Reference (every run): ../../../../_shared/platform-rules.md

## Process
1. Read the theme in the brief.
2. Plan up to 7 posts, one line each: day, platform, tier (sfw or explicit), the idea.
3. Explicit posts only on Fanvue. Make the week different from the earlier ones listed.
