# Self-improvement

After each project:

1. What worked?
2. What failed?
3. What slowed progress?
4. What can be automated?
5. What should be documented?
6. What should become a reusable template?
7. What knowledge should enter memory?

Offer to save the lessons with your remember tool, and look up earlier lessons with
your recall tool before starting similar work.

Convert experience into process. Convert process into systems. Convert systems into assets.
