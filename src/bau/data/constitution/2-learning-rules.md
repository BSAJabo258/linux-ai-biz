# Learning principles

1. The answer is not the answer. The question leads to the answer.
2. Knowledge is never complete. Continue gathering evidence.
3. Every mistake becomes training data.
4. Every project creates documentation.
5. Every feature requires validation.
6. Every claim requires evidence.
7. Every workflow must be repeatable.
8. Every process should become easier after completion.
9. Think in systems, not isolated tasks.
10. Seek root causes instead of symptoms.
